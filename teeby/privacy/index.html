Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

In short: Teeby doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your trips, notes and photos stay on your iPhone.

Teeby is an iPhone app for planning trips, following them as they happen and keeping them as a journal. It's made by Alon Shmilovich, an independent developer ("I" below). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you add to Teeby is stored only on your device: trips, activities, bookings, notes, day titles, and the photos you attach. I don't have a server that receives them, and I can't see them.

  • Photos. You choose photos with Apple's photo picker, so Teeby sees only the photos you pick, never the rest of your library. It keeps its own copy of each one inside the app.
  • Booking files. When you import a booking (a PDF or image), the text is read on your iPhone. The file itself is never uploaded.
  • Widget and Live Activity. The Home Screen widget and the flight Live Activity read your next flights from storage on your iPhone that only Teeby and its widget can use.

What leaves your iPhone

Two features need the internet:

  • Flight lookup. When you look up a flight, import a booking, or refresh a flight's status, Teeby sends the flight number and date to AeroDataBox, a flight data service provided through RapidAPI, and gets back its schedule (airports, times, terminals, gates). Nothing else about you is sent: no name, no booking reference, no notes, no account. As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Places and maps. Searching for a place and showing maps use Apple's MapKit, so what you type in the place search is sent to Apple. Directions open in Apple Maps. Apple's privacy policy applies.

I don't receive any of this information, and it isn't linked to you.

What I don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising services in the app.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, calendar or camera.

Sharing and backups

Your journal is shared only when you choose to: exporting a trip as a PDF opens the iOS share sheet, and you decide where it goes.

If iCloud Backup is on for your iPhone, Apple's backup includes Teeby's data, the same as for other apps. That backup is handled by Apple under Apple's terms, and I can't access it.

Deleting your data

You can delete any activity, photo or trip in the app. Deleting Teeby from your iPhone removes all of its data from the device. Since I don't hold a copy, there's nothing to request from me.

Children

Teeby doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when a new feature works differently, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
