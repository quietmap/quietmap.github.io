Need help or found a problem? Email quietmap.apps@gmail.com. I read every message.

It helps to include your iPhone model, the iOS version, and Teeby's version (shown at the top of Teeby's Settings tab). A screenshot helps too.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only. Teeby has no account and no server that keeps your trips. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I keep my trips safe?

Turn on iCloud Backup for your iPhone (Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Backup). It includes Teeby's trips, notes and photos, and restores them when you set up a new iPhone from that backup. Deleting Teeby from your iPhone deletes its data, so keep a backup before you do.

A flight lookup didn't find my flight

Check the flight number and the departure date. Flight data covers flights in the coming months and the recent past, so a flight far in the future or long ago may not be found yet. You can always fill in the flight yourself and edit it later.

An imported booking has the wrong time

Booking documents differ between airlines. When a flight can't be confirmed with flight data, Teeby shows it under "Check before adding" so you can review it. You can edit any flight after adding it.

The Live Activity doesn't appear

  • Make sure Live Activities are allowed: iPhone Settings → Teeby → Live Activities.
  • In Teeby's Settings tab, "Live Activity for upcoming flights" should be on.
  • It starts automatically when you open Teeby within 4 hours of a flight's departure. You can also start it from the flight's screen with Track Live.

The widget shows old information

The widget updates when you open Teeby. Open the app once after changing a flight, and the widget will follow.
